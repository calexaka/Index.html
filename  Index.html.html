🔍 IBS Help and Resources

From Process of Elimination: A Gut Detective's IBS Journal, by Kathleen Holt

See a doctor soon if you notice any of these:
  • Blood in your stool or black, tarry stool
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Fever, or symptoms that wake you up at night
  • New or changing symptoms, especially after age 50
  • Severe pain, or signs of dehydration
